## Known issues left on purpose

The final reviews passed both slices as ready to merge, and these were deferred. None blocks anything. Paths are under
`.camarones/`, and line numbers are for `9d56d50`.

**Pair canvas S2**

- `desktop/renderer/src/terminals.js` `shown()`: removing a unit's newer failed terminal brings back its older failed
  one, with a blank screen after a remount. The comment there says an older one never comes back.
- `lib/agents.py:159`: a window that fails to open leaves its unit refused for 30 s as "already open in a window". End
  the record when the open fails.
- Accepted cost: a remount within 2 s of a terminal query answers that query twice.
- The server handles SIGTERM but not SIGHUP on POSIX, and `select.select` has the `FD_SETSIZE` ceiling.

**Dynamic ADRs S1**

- `lib/adr_doc.py` `_status_line` and `_inline` read an inline `status:` outside the preamble, including in fenced YAML.
  Only legacy ADRs with an unknown status reach this.
- `lib/adr_doc.py:52` `_SKELETON_RE` (and its JS mirror) also matches `<https://…>` and `<br>`, so a link-only
  *Considered options* is replaced on accept.
- The English CLI message names the Spanish labels ("Decisiones › Revisar en sesión").
- `_affected` skips wiki pages and `covering` does not, so a wiki page can show the banner without being in *Docu
  afectada*.
- Kit-wide: pages that are symlinks pointing outside `docs/` are still read (writes are refused).
- `adr.py` (about 740 lines) and `Review.jsx` should be split at the next change.

**Where the detail lives.** The review ledgers are git-ignored and exist only on this machine, in
`.superpowers/sdd/` inside the `ecstatic-mendeleev-58a65e` and `dynamic-adrs-agents-a4a767` worktrees, under
`.claude/worktrees/`. They hold every ruling and about 80 minor ADR findings. Removing those worktrees deletes them, so
copy them out first if you want them.
